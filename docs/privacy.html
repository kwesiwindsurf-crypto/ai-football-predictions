Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for AI Scorecast, published by Xantechs Innovation. Legal documents are dry, so this one is written plainly and describes only what the app genuinely does with your information.

The short version: AI Scorecast has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number, and we have no idea who you are. There are no ads in this app and no advertising SDK, so we do not collect or use an Advertising ID. Anything you save inside the app stays on your device.

1. What We Collect & Why

The app needs a little information to fetch predictions and deliver alerts. Here is the complete list — nothing is collected outside it:

Stored on your device only

  • Your app preferences: theme, accent colour, time and odds format, default tab, minimum-confidence filter, and which alerts you want. These keep the app the way you left it.
  • Your tracked picks: if you use the tracker to log predictions you are following, those entries are written to your device's own app storage. They are not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • A local copy of the fixture and prediction data: the app caches the upcoming matches, live scores and AI predictions it has already downloaded so it can open instantly, including on a poor connection.

Sent to our backend

  • Requests for match data: the app asks our servers for fixtures, odds, live scores and predictions. These requests carry the league and date you are looking at — no name, no email, no advertising identifier, and no content from your tracker.

Handled by Google (Firebase)

  • Notifications: to deliver goal, kickoff and prediction alerts, Firebase Messaging issues your device a registration token. That token is how a message finds your phone. Turn notifications off in the app or in Android settings and no token is used.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics records diagnostics when the app stops unexpectedly — the device model, OS version, app version and the technical fault trace. This is how we fix crashes we could never reproduce ourselves.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics logs aggregate, anonymous app usage so we know which features are worth building. It is not tied to your identity because there is no identity to tie it to.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your location.
  • Contacts, camera, microphone, files or photos.
  • Your name, email address or phone number.
  • An Advertising ID, and any third-party advertising identifier of any kind.
  • Payment or card details — the app sells nothing and has no in-app purchases.
  • Your browsing history or data from other apps.

4. Who We Share Data With

We never sell your personal data, and there is nothing personal to sell. We share a limited amount of technical information with Google solely because they run the infrastructure the app depends on:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — delivers your notifications.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — collects crash diagnostics so we can fix them.
  • Google Firebase Analytics — collects anonymous usage statistics.
  • Our own hosting providers (Cloudflare and Render) run the servers that answer the app's data requests and hold standard server request logs.

Google's handling of this data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. We do not use any ad network or ad mediation platform.

5. Your Business Is Your Business

We cannot read your tracker. The predictions and picks you log live only in your device's private app storage. The app never transmits them, so we have no copy, no view, and nothing to hand over.

6. Managing and Deleting Your Data

⚠️ Please note: because the app holds no account and no server-side record of you, there is no account to delete and no request for us to action. Your data lives on your phone, so removing it is done on your phone. We cannot recover it for you afterwards.

🗂️ Option A: Clear Everything at Once

How to do it: Android Settings → Apps → AI Scorecast → Storage → Clear storage.

What gets deleted: your preferences, your logged picks, and the cached fixture and prediction data.

When it happens: immediately.

📱 Option B: Uninstall the App

How to do it: press and hold the AI Scorecast icon → Uninstall.

What gets deleted: everything the app stored on your device. Your notification registration token stops being usable as the app's data goes with it.

When it happens: immediately.

🔕 Option C: Just Stop the Alerts

How to do it: Settings → Apps → AI Scorecast → Notifications → off, or switch individual alert types off in the app's own settings screen.

What gets deleted: nothing — it stops future messages from reaching your device.

Crash and analytics data already sent to Google falls under Google's own retention and deletion controls; you can reset your advertising ID or contact us and we will point you at the right Google form.

7. Keeping Things Secure

Every request between the app and our servers travels over HTTPS with TLS, which covers fixtures, live scores and predictions. Data at rest stays in your device's private app storage, which Android isolates from other apps. We collect no credentials, so there are no credentials to leak.

8. Predictions Disclaimer

AI Scorecast provides automated football analytics, probability models and team insights for informational purposes only. We do not accept bets and do not operate a gambling service. Odds ratings and predictions are statistical probabilities and do not guarantee future match outcomes.

9. Age Requirements

Because the app discusses odds and prediction tracking, it is designed for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly direct this app at children and do not collect personal information from anyone under 13.
